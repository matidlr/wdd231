NP Membership

NP Membership is designed for nonprofit organizations that want to connect with the local business community at no annual cost.

  • No annual membership fee
  • Business directory listing
  • Networking opportunities
  • Community event participation

Bronze Membership

Bronze Membership provides additional opportunities for small businesses that want to increase their visibility.

  • All NP Membership benefits
  • Business workshops and training
  • Monthly Chamber newsletter
  • Discounted event registration

Silver Membership

Silver Membership offers greater promotional opportunities and increased business exposure.

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • Advertising opportunities
  • Social media promotion
  • Special event discounts
  • Business networking sessions

Gold Membership

Gold Membership is our highest level and provides maximum visibility, advertising, and networking opportunities.

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Homepage Spotlight advertising
  • VIP networking events
  • Premium advertising placement
  • Priority sponsorship opportunities
  • Featured business promotions
