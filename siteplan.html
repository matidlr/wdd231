SmileFlow Site Plan

Dental Practice Management System

Site Name

SmileFlow

The name SmileFlow was chosen because it represents a smooth and organized workflow for dental clinics. It is memorable, professional, and directly related to dentistry.

Suggested Domain: smileflow.app

Site Purpose

SmileFlow is a cloud-based application that helps dentists manage appointments, patient records, dental history, odontograms, medical files, appointment reminders, and treatment information. The system improves clinic organization while reducing administrative work.

Scenarios

  • How can I review a patient's dental history before today's appointment?
  • Can I upload X-rays and treatment photos to a patient's record?
  • How do I automatically remind patients about upcoming appointments?
  • Where can I view today's schedule and available appointment times?
